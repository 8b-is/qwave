How the grid learned to flex

A look at demand-side response, five years on.

The first large-scale demand response programme enrolled forty thousand homes in the first winter. Participation grew in steps: ten thousand in year two, twenty-five thousand by year three.

Households that shifted their evening load by one hour reduced the network's winter peak by an average of 3.1 percent. The effect was largest in regions with electric heating.
