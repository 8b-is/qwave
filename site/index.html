Meet Qwave. A WebKit browser with tabs, a choice of search engines, and optional tools to help you work with the pages you choose.

Get started with TestFlight ↗Your data, explained
01 / BROWSE

Find your next page.

Open tabs, move between pages, and search with Ecosia or DuckDuckGo.

02 / CHOOSE

Set your own pace.

Network search suggestions are optional. Explore settings to choose how you browse.

03 / REMEMBER

Work with your pages.

Optional Memory Wave tools offer local processing where supported. A remote AI provider is a separate choice.

A browser in progress. Your feedback matters.

Qwave 2.0.4 is available to invited internal TestFlight testers on Mac and iPhone. Public availability has not been announced.

Automatic page translation and updated Wave panels are in development and are not part of the current 2.0.4 beta.
