This file accompanies mlkem768_acvp_vectors.json.

mlkem768_acvp_vectors.json is a verbatim subset of the official NIST ACVP
ML-KEM (FIPS 203) demo vectors published by the National Institute of
Standards and Technology at https://github.com/usnistgov/ACVP-Server.
As a work of the United States Government it is not subject to copyright
protection in the United States (17 U.S.C. 105); NIST asserts no copyright
over the ACVP test vector data.

Denominator (counted directly from the two files at the commits above):
parameterSet "ML-KEM-768" has 80 cases in total, spread over five test
groups.

  encapDecap       2  AFT       encapsulation              25

tgId 9 (decapsulationKeyCheck) is deliberately skipped in full — there is no
`validateDecapsulationKey` API to run it against, and 10 x 4.8 KB of `dk`
exceeds the fixture size budget. Excluding it, 70 cases are runnable in
principle.

Selection: 29 cases are committed. Against the four groups actually drawn
from that is 29 of 70; against every ML-KEM-768 case upstream, 29 of 80.

Fidelity: every hex payload is byte-identical to the upstream file,
lowercased. No value was recomputed, reordered or adjusted. Two structural
(non-value) changes were made, and they are the only ones:

  1. Upstream groups cases under "testGroups"/"tests" with a numeric "tcId".
     Here each group is a top-level array (keyGen, encapsulation,
     decapsulation, encapsulationKeyCheck) and "tcId" is rendered as a
     "name" string ("acvp-keyGen-tc26" etc.) so a failing Swift Testing case
     names its upstream case. The tc number is preserved verbatim.
  2. In the encapsulationKeyCheck cases, upstream's boolean field
     "testPassed" is RENAMED to "valid". The boolean value is copied
     unchanged; only the key differs. (Upstream's "deferred" flag, and the
     "dk"/"ek" fields not needed by a given group, are dropped.)

The full upstream files are ~2 MB, which is why a subset is committed rather
than the whole thing.

These vectors are READ-ONLY TRUTH. A failing case means the implementation
under test is wrong. Do not edit, regenerate, filter to the passing subset,
or delete a failing case.

Not used as a conformance oracle:

  github.com/C2SP/CCTV ML-KEM "unluckysample" vectors were evaluated as a
  supplement for the SampleNTT rejection-sampling overrun path. They were
  rejected as a known-answer oracle because they are FIPS 203 *ipd*-era:
  in ML-KEM-768.txt the public seed rho equals SHA3-512(d)[0:32], whereas
  final FIPS 203 (Algorithm 16) specifies (rho, sigma) = G(d || k), so the
  file's ek/dk/c cannot be reproduced by a conformant implementation. Only
  the unlucky rho itself — 5c3c6fe50d06fc1bbbffafc56ab7050f2773ee8ef8d28c
  a4b97b43c8d7202e71, which needs 384 12-bit draws (576 XOF bytes) before
  SampleNTT accepts 256 coefficients — is reused, and only in a property
  test (MLKEM768VectorSuite.unluckyRhoNeedsMoreThanOneSqueeze), never as a
  known-answer test.
